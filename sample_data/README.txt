SYNTHETIC sample data generated by make_sample_data.py from known laws plus noise.
They exist only to demonstrate the app; they are not real measurements.
Physics files (pendulum, ideal gas, spring, electrical power) feed the Scientific Discovery Lab.
coffee_shop.csv and study_habits.csv are everyday examples for the Everyday Data Lab (study_habits has a skewed column
and a sweet-spot variable; coffee_shop has diminishing returns and a distractor).
